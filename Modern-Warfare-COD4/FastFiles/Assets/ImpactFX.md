# Impact FX Asset

Effects triggered on projectile/bullet impacts with surfaces.

> **Note:** Asset type IDs vary by platform. See [AssetTypes.md](FastFiles/AssetTypes.md) for the full list.

## Overview

Impact FX assets define the visual and audio effects when projectiles hit different surface types.

## Surface Types

Different effects for:
- Metal
- Wood
- Concrete
- Dirt
- Water
- Flesh
- Glass
