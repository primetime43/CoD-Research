Game Server Commands

O "Print Killfeed"
< "Print Center"
o "Vision"
; "Print Killfeed"
^ "Dvar"
5 "Kick everyone from the lobby" (Exmple 5 ^5GTFO)
7 "Freeze PS3" Exmple:7 30 90
d "Blur" Exmple: d 10000 10000 = Blur 10 seconds
