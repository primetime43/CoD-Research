# Impact FX Asset

Effects triggered on projectile/bullet impacts with surfaces.

## Overview

Impact FX assets define the visual and audio effects that occur when bullets, explosions, or other projectiles hit different surface types.

## Features

- Surface-specific effects (metal, wood, concrete, flesh, etc.)
- Particle effects for debris
- Decal placement
- Sound effects on impact
- Different effects for entry vs exit wounds

## Surface Types

Impact effects vary based on the surface material:

| Surface | Examples |
|---------|----------|
| Metal | Sparks, metallic sounds |
| Wood | Splinters, wood chips |
| Concrete | Dust, stone fragments |
| Glass | Shatter effects |
| Flesh | Blood effects |
| Water | Splash effects |
| Dirt/Sand | Dust clouds |
